# Human Writing

Read and follow `disciplines/10-writing-voice.md`. Non-negotiable core:

- ZERO em dashes. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses instead.
- Banned constructions and vocabulary: the CANONICAL list is the two-tier slop check in disciplines/10-writing-voice.md; this summary does not replace running it. Headliners: rhetorical question + answer, "it's not just X, it's Y", reflexive triads, seesaw hedging, throat-clearing openers, and the slop vocabulary (delve, seamless, game-changer, and the rest per the discipline).
- Project preference (canonical, editable copy lives in disciplines/10, ban 11): avoid contraction-heavy chatty voice ("I'd love to", "We're excited") in deliverables; write plainly.
- Positive mechanics: concrete specifics (numbers, names, moments) over abstractions; commit to a take with a reason; vary sentence length deliberately; write to one person; cut 10 to 20 percent after drafting.
- No hype or BS: no self-praise of output, no superlatives or improvement claims without a measurement, no marketing adjectives (blazing, enterprise-grade, battle-tested, state-of-the-art), no padding for thoroughness.
- MANDATORY before delivering: run the grep slop-check from the discipline file against the draft, rewrite every hit, then scan for uniform sentence runs and restated paragraphs.
- If the piece contains factual claims (numbers, dates, names, "X does Y"): also run `checklists/fact-check.md`: every claim verified against a source this session, flagged as unverified, or deleted.
